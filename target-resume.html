Full Resume Package
What the Paid Package Includes

The full resume package includes five versions of your resume, each designed for a different situation. Credits are used when you generate the full package.

ATS Resume
Use when uploading to online job portals and applicant tracking systems. Clean formatting so the system can read your experience accurately.
Polished Resume
Use when emailing a recruiter, hiring manager, or professional contact directly. Clean, editable, professional layout.
Presentation Resume
Use for interviews, networking, portfolio packets, and career fairs where you are physically handing someone your resume. Designed to feel more polished in print.
Plain Text Version
Use when a job application asks you to paste your resume into a form. Removes formatting so the content stays clean and readable.
Canva Prompt — Free Bonus
Every package includes a custom Canva prompt generated for your role and template style. Use it if you want to experiment with your own visual layout in Canva while keeping the resume content and strategy intact. Canva can help you design. Target Resume helps you know what to say.
Print Guidance
For the Presentation Resume
Use matte white or natural white paper. A 24 lb or 32 lb paper stock gives the resume a more substantial feel than standard copy paper. Avoid glossy paper — it creates glare and can feel too promotional. Print in high quality mode and bring 3 to 5 copies to interviews, networking events, career fairs, or portfolio meetings.

Resume Package Notes
Editing Tips
These templates balance readability, spacing, and professional layout. If text spills onto another page or wraps strangely, the solution is usually to shorten the text, not shrink the font.
Recommended Limits
Professional Summary: 3-5 lines
Core Competencies: 9-15 skills max
Bullets per role: 3-5 (5-7 for most recent)
Bullet length: 1-2 lines
Font size: no smaller than 10pt body
Page count: 1 page early career, 2 pages experienced
Common Fixes
Spilling onto another page: shorten summary or reduce bullets
Awkward wrapping: keep bullets to 1-2 lines
Looks crowded: remove lower-priority details, not the font size
Looks strange in a portal: switch to the ATS version
Layout shifts after editing: paste without formatting
