Applied Tool: The Rebound Effect

REBOUND
RADAR

What to learn next while the market is still catching up.

Technology does not only compress and remove old work. It also creates new demand around adoption, training, oversight, integration, trust, repair, and transition. Rebound Radar helps you spot where that demand is appearing now.

Some opportunities here may become lasting careers. Others may be valuable long-term skills. Some may be temporary bridge roles that fade once tools become easier or built into standard software. The goal is not to predict the future perfectly. The goal is to help you see what is worth learning and testing while the window is open.
