SAFE CAREERS LIST

Explore career paths with stronger durability signals in an AI-changing economy.

No career is completely safe from technological change. This list ranks careers by relative pressure: how much the work depends on human judgment, physical presence, trust, licensing, unpredictable real-world conditions, and emotional complexity. The Obsolete category documents professions that technology has already fully replaced.
