MAP

Find a new direction, or build toward one while keeping your current job.

Changing direction does not always mean quitting everything or going back to school full-time. Roadmap helps you choose between two practical paths: finding job options that may already fit your current experience, or building a step-by-step plan around your work schedule so you can move toward something new over time.

This tool is built for people who are still working and need to move at a realistic pace. Choose Find Job Paths to discover roles you may already be close to, or choose Build a Skill Plan to create a step-by-step learning plan that fits around your current job, schedule, and budget.
