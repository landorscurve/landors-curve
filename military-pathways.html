Sources and Methodology
How This Tool Works

Military-to-Civilian Pathways is designed to synthesize publicly available workforce and military occupation data. It uses O*NET occupational crosswalks, BLS labor market projections, SOC classification standards, and published military occupation data to map military experience to civilian career options. Landor's Curve adds its own AI-assisted interpretation, skill transfer analysis, and pathway weighting. This tool does not represent or replace official guidance from O*NET, BLS, the Department of Defense, DMDC, or any government agency. Results are directional estimates.
