Section 1
Why Free Matters

The free tools on Landor's Curve are designed to be genuinely useful, even if someone never pays. Career insight should not be reserved only for institutions with large budgets.

Free access helps users explore the platform, understand how the tools work, and walk away with clearer questions, better direction, and a stronger sense of their options.

Section 2
Why Paid Tiers Will Exist

Deeper reports require more resources. Expanded analysis, larger source-layer checks, formatted documents, resume packages, email delivery, and continued development all require real infrastructure and operating costs.

Paid credits will support deeper tools while helping keep the free version useful and accessible.

Section 3
Lightweight by Design

Landor's Curve is intentionally lightweight. When paid tiers launch, the goal is to keep the system simple: no unnecessary profiles, no selling personal data, and no complicated account system.

The goal is to use only what is needed to deliver results, manage credits, and send secure access links.

Section 8
Common Questions
Do I need an account?+
The goal is to keep Landor's Curve lightweight. When paid tiers launch, access will be managed with email and secure links wherever possible, instead of requiring unnecessary user profiles.
When are credits used?+
Credits are intended to be used only when a full report or finished document is generated. Exploring, previewing, uploading, and adjusting should remain free.
Why charge at all?+
Deeper analysis, source checks, report generation, document formatting, hosting, and ongoing development require real resources. Paid tiers help support those costs while keeping the free tools useful.
Are recommendations paid placements?+
No. Landor's Curve is not designed around paid placements from schools, employers, training programs, or recruiters. The goal is objective analysis based on available information, user inputs, and the structure of the analysis.
Will prices change?+
Possibly. This page reflects the planned beta pricing structure. Pricing may be adjusted as tools, reports, and infrastructure are refined.
