SAFE CAREERS LIST

Explore career paths with stronger durability signals in an AI-changing economy.

No career is completely safe from technological change. This list ranks careers by relative pressure: how much the work depends on human judgment, physical presence, trust, licensing, unpredictable real-world conditions, and emotional complexity.

The Durability Score is a directional estimate, not a prediction. It reflects visible durability signals such as licensing, physical-world execution, human judgment, trust, emotional complexity, regulation, and adaptability. Use it as a thinking tool, not a guarantee.
