CAREER ASSESSMENT

Use your existing experience to identify smarter career directions and adjacent opportunities.

As the curve accelerates displacement, new opportunities rise on the other side. Your experience and skill set are more valuable than you think. Enter what you know and how long you have known it. We will calculate career options you may have never considered, and some that did not even exist until now.

Upload your resume or enter your background to see the skills, strengths, and career options already hidden inside what you have done. This page is designed to assess what you may already qualify for without assuming you need more school, a new degree, or a major investment in yourself.

Career Assessment helps you understand what you may already qualify for before assuming you need to start over. It looks at your experience, estimates your strongest areas of expertise, and suggests career options that may already fit your background.

Your Skills and Experience
How this works
You can add the same skill multiple times with different time periods. For example, if you taught part-time from 2005 to 2010 and then full-time from 2015 to 2023, add Teaching twice with different hours and years. We add all your hours together across every entry and calculate your true expertise level. It is not about one straight line - it is about the total hours you have invested over your lifetime. At 10,000 hours you reach Master level. Most people are surprised how much they actually know.
